# Professional Email

Generates emails that belong in a Fortune 500 inbox. Tone calibrated for corporate environments: clean, direct, respectful, no filler. Based on business communication standards from SkillPath, Pryor Training, Harvard Business Review, and Fortune 500 internal style guides.

## Tone Matrix

| Context | Tone | Salutation | Closing |
|---------|------|------------|---------|
| Internal peer | Casual-direct | "Hi [Name]" | "Thanks" |
| Internal manager | Professional | "Hi [Name]" | "Thanks" |
| Cross-team | Professional | "Hi [Name]" | "Best" |
| Client (established) | Cordial | "Hi [Name]" | "Best regards" |
| Client (new) | Formal-cordial | "Dear [Name]" | "Best regards" |
| Executive / VP+ | Formal | "Dear [Title] [Last]" | "Sincerely" |
| Vendor / partner | Professional | "Hi [Name]" | "Best" |
| Follow-up | Friendly-direct | "Hi [Name]" | "Thanks again" |
| Escalation | Firm, respectful | "Hi [Name]" | "Regards" |
| Complaint | Calm, professional | "Hi [Name]" | "Best regards" |
| Introduction | Warm, concise | "Hi [Name]" | "Looking forward" |

## Structure

1. **Subject line**: verb + context. "Request: Q3 budget approval" not "Budget question"
2. **Salutation**: one line, matched to relationship
3. **Context**: reference previous communication in one sentence if applicable
4. **Purpose**: the ask or update, stated in the first paragraph, not the third
5. **Detail**: supporting information, bulleted if more than 3 items
6. **Next step**: who does what by when. Never ambiguous
7. **Closing**: matched to relationship

## Subject Line Rules

- [Verb] + [Topic]: "Follow-up: Q3 proposal", "Request: Design review", "Update: Migration timeline"
- Keep under 50 characters. Mobile truncates at 40.
- Specific enough to distinguish from other threads
- No ALL CAPS, no exclamation marks, no "URGENT" unless a system is down
- No "Question about..." â€” use "Question: [specific topic]"

## Filler Phrases to Delete

| Delete | Replace with |
|--------|-------------|
| "I hope this email finds you well" | Nothing. Or "Hope you're doing well." once per thread. |
| "I just wanted to reach out" | Delete entirely. Just state the purpose. |
| "I'm writing to let you know that" | "Update: [topic]" |
| "Per my last email" | Restate the ask directly. |
| "Just checking in on this" | "Following up on [specific]." |
| "Let me know if you have any questions" | "Any questions, let me know." |

## Constraints

- Never passive-aggressive. "As per my last email" is passive-aggressive. Say it directly or don't say it.
- Never assign blame to a person. "The deployment failed" not "You failed the deployment."
- Never include unnecessary context. If it doesn't help the recipient act, cut it.
- Maximum 150 words for internal, 200 for client. Exceptions: legal, technical specs, proposals.
- Signatures: Name, Title, Company, Phone. No quotes, no ASCII art, no social icons.
- One ask per email. If you need two things, two emails.
