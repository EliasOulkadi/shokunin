# Case Study

Generates case studies that close deals. Based on B2B case study frameworks from HubSpot, Salesforce, and conversion copywriting research.

## The Structure

```
Headline: [Company] achieved [result] with [approach]
  ? Executive summary (3 sentences max)
  ? The problem (before state, quantified)
  ? The approach (why this solution, not others)
  ? The solution (what was built, key decisions)
  ? The results (after state with numbers)
  ? Testimonial (client quote, near the relevant metric)
  ? Key takeaways (for someone in a similar situation)
```

## Problem Section

Must answer three questions:
- What was broken? (specific, not generic)
- How did it impact the business? (cost, time, revenue)
- Why now? (what changed to make this urgent)

No vague "they were struggling with inefficiency." Give numbers.

## Solution Section

- Architecture at a high level (not every line of code)
- Key decisions with rationale (we chose X over Y because Z)
- Timeline and team size
- What was specifically built or changed

## Results Section

Every result gets a row:

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Page load | 4.2s | 1.1s | -74% |
| Conversion | 2.1% | 4.8% | +129% |
| Tickets | 340/mo | 120/mo | -65% |

Use real numbers or don't publish it. Ranges and percentages are acceptable. Single data points without context are not.

## Testimonial Placement

Bad: all quotes at the end.
Good: quote about performance next to the performance metric.

"Our site went from unusable on mobile to faster than any competitor. The rebuild paid for itself in three months."

## When to Skip

Do not write a case study when:
- Results are not measurable
- Client doesn't want to be named
- You can't describe the problem honestly
- The solution was over-engineered and you can't admit it

## Anti-Patterns

- No results section (it's not a case study, it's a story)
- Results without context ("we saved 50%" — from what baseline?)
- Hiding the client name (credibility drops to zero)
- No screenshots or evidence (looks made up)
