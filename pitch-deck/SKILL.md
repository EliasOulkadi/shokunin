# Pitch Deck

Generates pitch deck content that tells a compelling story. Based on the 10-slide structure from Sequoia Capital, Y Combinator, and venture-backed pitch analysis.

## The 10 Slides

1. **Title**: problem or vision in one line
2. **Problem**: what's broken, who feels it, how bad
3. **Solution**: your product, simply stated
4. **Market**: TAM, SAM, SOM with sources
5. **Product**: demo or key screenshots, not feature list
6. **Traction**: revenue, users, growth rate, retention
7. **Business model**: how you make money, unit economics
8. **Competition**: landscape map (2x2), your moat
9. **Team**: relevant experience, why this team wins
10. **Ask**: what you need, what it funds, milestones

## Story Arc

| Slide | Emotion | Question it answers |
|-------|---------|--------------------|
| 1-2 | Pain | Why should I care? |
| 3-4 | Hope | How big is this? |
| 5-6 | Proof | Does it work? |
| 7-8 | Trust | Can it scale? |
| 9-10 | Action | Why now, why you? |

## Content Rules

- One slide, one idea. No exceptions.
- Maximum 20 words per slide (for slides 1-4 and 7-10)
- Slides 5-6 (demo, traction) can have more detail
- No bullet points. Use short phrases.
- Font minimum: 24pt for content, 36pt for headlines
- Images > text for product slides

## Traction Metrics

Investor expectations by stage:

| Stage | Key metric | Benchmark |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Pre-seed | Problem validation | 50+ user interviews |
| Seed | Monthly active users | 10k+ MAU |
| Series A | Revenue | $1M+ ARR |
| Series B | Growth + retention | 3x YoY, >80% retention |
