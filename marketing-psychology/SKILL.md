Apply psychological principles to marketing, copy, and design. Based on Cialdini's 6 principles, cognitive biases, and conversion research.

## The 7 Universal Persuasion Principles (Cialdini + expanded)

| Principle | Description | Application |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Reciprocity | People give back what they receive | Free trials, valuable content upfront, bonuses |
| Scarcity | People want what is limited | Limited time, limited stock, exclusive access |
| Authority | People trust experts | Certifications, awards, media features, data |
| Consistency | People stick with past commitments | Progressive commitments, checkboxes, opt-ins |
| Liking | People say yes to people they like | Relatable brand voice, shared values, similarity |
| Social Proof | People follow others | Testimonials, user count, case studies, reviews |
| Unity | People trust "us" over "them" | Community, shared identity, membership language |

## Cognitive Biases for Marketing

| Bias | How it works | Marketing use |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Anchoring | First number sets the reference | Show higher price first, then your price |
| Decoy effect | Unattractive option makes another look better | 3 pricing tiers, middle is the offer |
| Loss aversion | Losses hurt more than gains feel good | "You are missing out" over "You will gain" |
| Framing | Context changes perception | "90% success" vs "10% failure" |
| Endowment | People value what they already have | Free trials, customization |
| Paradox of choice | More options = less action | Limit choices to 3 per decision |
| Peak-end rule | People judge experiences by peak and end | Make the best moment great, end strong |

## Anti-Patterns

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Fake scarcity | Destroys trust permanently | Only use genuine scarcity |
| Too many choices | Decision paralysis, lower conversion | Limit to 3 options maximum |
| No social proof | Low credibility | Add testimonials near the ask |
| Overwhelming claims | Skepticism, bounce | Back claims with specific data |
| Ignoring objections | Drop-off at decision point | Address objections before they arise |
