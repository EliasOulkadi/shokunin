Run structured brainstorming sessions. Alternates divergent and convergent thinking for maximum output without chaos.

## The Process

Round 1: Divergent (generate, no judgment)
Round 2: Convergent (organize, group, eliminate)
Round 3: Select (vote, prioritize, commit)

## Divergent Techniques

| Technique | How | Best for |
|-----------|-----|----------|
| Free association | Write everything, no filter | Warm-up, quantity |
| Brainwriting | Everyone writes silently, then passes to next person | Avoiding dominant voices |
| Reverse brainstorming | How would we make the problem worse? Then reverse | Breaking assumptions |
| Random word | Pick a random noun, force connections to the problem | Breaking mental blocks |
| SCAMPER prompts | Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to use, Eliminate, Reverse | Systematic exploration |

## Convergent Techniques

| Technique | How | Best for |
|-----------|-----|----------|
| Affinity mapping | Group ideas by theme | Large sets of ideas |
| Dot voting | Each person gets 3 votes | Quick prioritization |
| Impact/effort matrix | X: impact, Y: effort | Deciding what to execute |
| How-Now-Wow matrix | X: ease, Y: originality | Finding breakthrough ideas |

## Anti-Patterns

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Mixed divergent and convergent | Judgment kills ideas before they form | Separate phases explicitly |
| Dominant voices take over | Groupthink, missed ideas | Use brainwriting, round-robin |
| Too many people | Chaos, no progress | 4-8 people maximum |
| No follow-through | Ideas die after the session | Assign ownership and next steps |
