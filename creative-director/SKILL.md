Creative direction with structured ideation methodologies. Combines systematic innovation frameworks with creative brief development.

## Ideation Methodologies

| Methodology | Approach | Best for |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| SCAMPER | Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to use, Eliminate, Reverse | Product innovation, feature ideation |
| Design Thinking | Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test | Human-centered problem solving |
| TRIZ | 40 inventive principles, contradiction matrix | Technical problem solving |
| Bisociation | Connecting two unrelated frameworks | Breakthrough creative concepts |
| Synectics | Analogies, personal analogy, direct analogy | Creative campaigns, storytelling |
| SIT (Systematic Inventive Thinking) | Subtraction, division, multiplication, task unification, attribute dependency | Product and service innovation |

## Creative Brief Structure

1. Background: why are we doing this?
2. Objective: what specific outcome do we need?
3. Target audience: who are we talking to? (demographics + psychographics)
4. Insight: what does the audience believe or feel that we can leverage?
5. Single-minded proposition: the one thing we need them to know
6. Support: why they should believe it
7. Tone: how should it feel?
8. Mandatories: what must be included (logo, legal, timeline)

## Evaluation Criteria

| Axis | What it measures | Scale |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Originality | Has this been done before? | 1-10 |
| Relevance | Does it fit the brand and brief? | 1-10 |
| Impact | Will people remember and act? | 1-10 |
| Feasibility | Can we execute this? | 1-10 |

## Anti-Patterns

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| No single-minded proposition | Campaign tries to say everything and says nothing | Force one idea. Kill the rest. |
| Skipping audience insight | Creative that does not connect | Research before creating. |
| Too many stakeholders | Watered-down creative | Limit decision-makers to 3. |
| No evaluation criteria | Subjective "I like it / I don't" | Use the 4-axis evaluation. |
