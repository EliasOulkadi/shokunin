# Landing Craft

Generates landing pages designed for conversion. Combines aesthetic web design with performance optimization and conversion rate research.

## The Structure

Hero headline + sub + CTA, Social proof logos or metrics, Problem framed for empathy, Solution 3 steps max, Features grid 3-6 items, Testimonials real people, Pricing 3 tiers middle is the offer, FAQ answers objections, Final CTA same ask different context

Every section answers: what does the visitor need to hear right now to move to the next section?

## Design Palette

Dark #080808 or cream #f5f2ec. Never generic grey. Headline editorial serif at clamp(48px, 7vw, 96px). Body clean sans at 16px minimum. 8px spacing grid. Grain overlay at 0.03-0.06. Lucide or Heroicons, no emojis. Parallax or 3D on hero.

## Hero Rules

Value in 3 seconds. CTA visible without scroll. One CTA, not three. Headline under 10 words. Supporting visual.

## Social Proof

Near the top. Logo cloud, user count, recognizable testimonial, or award badge.

## Pricing

3 tiers. Middle is the offer. First anchors low, third makes middle look reasonable. Free enough to get value, limited to push upgrade. Pro is the real offer. Enterprise custom.

## Conversion Patterns

Risk reversal free trial or guarantee. Social proof near the CTA. Honest scarcity only. One primary action per section.

## Performance

Lighthouse over 90. FCP under 1.5s. No render-blocking resources above fold. Images lazy-loaded. Minimal JS.
